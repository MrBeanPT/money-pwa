Privacy notice — "Money" personal finance tool

This is a private, non-commercial tool used by a single person to keep their own personal finance records. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

Only the owner's own bank account information (account details, balances and transactions), and only after the owner has authorised access at their bank.

Where it goes

Data is retrieved through Enable Banking and stored only on the owner's own computer, in their personal finance file. It is not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for profiling or marketing.

This website

This static site hosts the tool's code. It sets no cookies, has no analytics and stores no financial data.

Withdrawing consent

Access can be revoked at any time at the bank or in Enable Banking; locally stored data can be deleted by the owner.
